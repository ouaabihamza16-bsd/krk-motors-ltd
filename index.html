Before you buy any used car

Good advice whoever you buy from. These are worth checking every time.

  • See the V5C registration document and check it matches the car.
  • Check the MOT history for free on GOV.UK.
  • Ask about service history and any previous repairs.
  • Check the car is not subject to outstanding finance.
  • Take a test drive and look the car over properly.
